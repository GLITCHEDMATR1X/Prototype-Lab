VECTOR WARS AUDIO

Shipped combat cues use OGG for reliable pygame-ce / SDL_mixer playback.
Player-owned replacement audio belongs outside the installation under:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\GLITCHED MATRIX\Vector Wars\custom_audio

Use matching folders under custom_audio\sfx for weapon and effect replacements.
Long music tracks belong under custom_audio\music\ambient.
WAV and OGG are recommended for short sound effects.

Release defaults:
- Packaged combat cues normalized to about -16 LUFS.
- Master SFX starts at 46%, with a 65% runtime safety cap.
- M mutes; - and + adjust player volume.
