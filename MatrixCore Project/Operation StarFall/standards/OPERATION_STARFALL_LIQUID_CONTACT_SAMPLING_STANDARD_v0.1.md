# Operation StarFall Liquid Contact / Sampling Standard v0.1

## Rule
Liquid surfaces are hazards and sample targets, not walkable/swimmable spaces.

## Titan liquids
Titan seas/lakes must be represented as liquid basins that cut into neighboring terrain on all sides. The terrain under any rendered liquid alpha must be clamped below the liquid plane so no transparent sheet appears over raised ground.

## Unit restrictions
- Rover body may approach the bank but may not touch liquid.
- Hover drone may fly above the bank or over the sampling line but must stay above the liquid clearance limit.
- Land rover and drone controls must project unsafe target points back to safe bank positions.
- Drill holes cannot be placed inside liquid contact zones.

## Sampling
Liquid targets are tested remotely from a dry bank point using the sample tether. Hotspots may have a liquid sample coordinate and a separate safe drill/anchor coordinate.

## Factual information
In-game facts must stay grounded in real Saturn-system science. Titan liquid callouts should describe methane/ethane lakes/seas, not water oceans. Enceladus callouts should describe plume/ocean chemistry. Mimas and Iapetus facts should use their known dominant features.
