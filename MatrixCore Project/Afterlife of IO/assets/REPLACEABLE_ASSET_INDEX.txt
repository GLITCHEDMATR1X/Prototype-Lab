RULES
- PNG replacements may be any reasonable dimensions unless a local README says otherwise; runtime fit/scaling preserves aspect ratio where applicable.
- Transparent PNGs are preferred for characters, particles, story figures, anchors, FX, and overlay ornaments.
- Missing/corrupt presentation replacements are nonfatal and use existing fallbacks where supported.
- Missing/corrupt fonts are nonfatal and fall back to a system or pygame default font.
- Replacement art must never control gameplay state, collision, saves, progression, or damage values.
