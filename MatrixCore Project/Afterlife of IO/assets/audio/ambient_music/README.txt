AFTERLIFE OF IO — MUSIC ROLES

Drop soundtrack files directly in this folder.
Supported playlist extensions: .ogg, .mp3, .wav, .flac

Playback rules:
- Tracks play alphabetically by filename.
- The playlist loops forever and returns to the first track after the last.
- One track by itself loops forever.
- Music fades in on game launch.
- Empty folder or unavailable audio device will not stop the game from launching.

Tip: prefix filenames with numbers to control order, e.g.:
01_wind.ogg
02_ruins.ogg
03_descent.ogg
