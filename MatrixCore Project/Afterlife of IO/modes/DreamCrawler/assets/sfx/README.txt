Dream Crawler replaceable SFX

Cue stems loaded by the game:
  spawn   - room/materialization arrival
  exit    - stairs/monster-clear transition
  step    - player and nearby co-op footsteps
  caught  - crawler contact/downed sting
  hit     - one-use weapon impact
  loot    - treasure or weapon pickup
  alert   - crawler commits to chasing the human player

You may replace any cue with WAV, OGG, or MP3 using the same stem.
The generated WAV files are original procedural placeholders created for this build.
