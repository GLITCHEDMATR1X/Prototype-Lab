THE INDIGO GIANT - PASS 61  NYX, ORBIT, CRIMSON - THE ETCHINGS - GLEEBS AND THE END OF REDACTED
===============================================================================================

THE NAMES
---------
  - Nyx is the Indigo Giant. Orbit is the human (you). Crimson is the red giant.
  - The planet is REDACTED: those who left it scratched its name from every chart, so no one
    would look for it.
  - Every line the player reads uses them:
      - messages
      - HUD labels: "nyx · staying", "crimson · stalking · 72 m", "you are Nyx", "riding Nyx"
      - prompts
      - F1 help and the keys page: "be Nyx / be Orbit", "Nyx: punch"
      - hints, places' stories, the journal, the ending
      - the audio README
  - The title stays "The Indigo Giant".
  - Code names (variables, sound keys, save keys, clip names) are unchanged, so saves and your
    controls.json carry over.
  - Fixed on the way: the spoken lines at the bottom of the screen were always drawn in dark ink,
    so at night they were nearly unreadable. They now use the night ink (pale), with a soft
    shadow.


THE ETCHINGS - little notes of lore
-----------------------------------
14 standing stones, cut with rows of small angular glyphs.
  - Where they stand:
      - the first ~60 m from where the journey starts, the last ~9.4 km out
      - spread all round (the golden angle)
      - always on open, fairly level sand: never in a shell, a place or water, and nothing is
        placed on top of them later
  - How they look:
      - by day the marks are dark cuts in pale stone
      - at night they glow cyan, and a soft glimmer over the stone shows it from ~250 m
      - both broad faces are cut
  - Hold E beside one (1 s) to read it. The words stay on screen ~9 s, with "etching 3 of 14 · J >
    notes". A read etching dims a little; you can read it again.
  - J > notes (a third journal page) lists every etching you have read, in order from the start.
  - Read nearest first, they tell:
      1  NYX, "the night that keeps you warm"
      2  ORBIT, the small one who never strays from Nyx
      3  CRIMSON, and the light it burns at night
      4  Crimson is stronger than Nyx, but not wise: it eats everything, bitter or sweet
      5  this world's name scratched out; over the scars: REDACTED
      6  the lights in the sky that stopped coming
      7  those who left did not want it found; they wrote REDACTED in its place
      8  the rivers, then the green: REDACTED is dying
      9  Nyx's kind were many; one is left, and one small thing beside it
      10 Crimson's kind ate the pale flower because it was there, and did not get up
      11 a figure of thin bright lines with long ears, in a beam from the sky: GLEEBS
      12 Gleebs took the last small ones away, and promised to listen
      13 when Crimson lies down for good the sky will answer; go into the light together; it will
         not take one who is alone
      14 REDACTED is dying. Do not stay. Do not stay alone.
  - Saved with the journey.


THE END - GLEEBS COMES
----------------------
Crimson falls the same way as before: find the pale bloom, poison a blood branch, let Crimson eat
it. Then, instead of the old "the red one sleeps" card, the sky answers.

  ARRIVING (about 40 s; you can move)
    - A beam of light falls out of the sky ~58 m ahead of where you are looking, on clear sand.
    - Gleebs flickers into being in it as a hologram, 26 m tall (1.3 x Nyx):
        - the Utopia Vision model (assets/gleebs/Gleebs_Game.bam), looping its Idle
        - cyan, with scanlines crawling up through it, glitching now and then
        - hanging just above the sand, breathing
      Its light falls on the sand, on Nyx, on you and on Crimson's body.
    - It speaks (subtitled, with a soft voice sound):
        "Nyx. Orbit. I heard you, at last."
        "No one comes to REDACTED any more. They scratched its name from every chart, so no one
         would look."
        "This world is dying. I can carry you both away from it."
        "Crimson ate what was bitter, because it was there. It was strong, but never wise. It
         stays with REDACTED."
        "Step into my light together, when you are ready. I will wait."

  WAITING (as long as you like; saved)
    - Walk into the light (within 9 m of the beam):
        - with Nyx beside you (Nyx within 30 m of the beam, and not knocked out): hold E 2 s -
          "leave REDACTED with Gleebs"
        - alone: "Not alone. Bring Nyx into the light with you."

  LEAVING (a 16 s scene; no control, the world holds still, the HUD steps aside)
    - Nyx and Orbit are drawn into the beam and rise ~70 m up it, as the beam flares.
    - Cut to Crimson, lying where it fell, with the beam behind it:
      "Crimson stays behind, lying where it fell. It was strong, but it was never wise enough to
      follow."
    - The beam goes out.

  THE CARD: "they left REDACTED"
    - Nyx and Orbit went up into the light with Gleebs.
    - Crimson stayed. It was strong, but never wise, and it perished with REDACTED.
    - your journey's numbers
    - No one will look for this world again.
    - new journey / quit
    The journey is over: the card comes back if closed, and loading this journey shows it again.

Saves:
  - Gleebs' place and state are saved.
  - Saved while it was still speaking: it comes down again in the same place.
  - A journey saved before this pass with Crimson already dead: Gleebs comes a few seconds after
    loading.


HOW IT IS MADE
--------------
  lore.py    LoreMixin:
               - the stones: one shared flat-shaded mesh, instanced
               - the glyph textures: drawn into a PNMImage at start-up
               - the cut / glow cards, the glimmer
               - reading (the E chain), the notes page lines, saves
  gleebs.py  GleebsMixin (before EndgameMixin, so its show_ending starts the arrival):
               - the beam: two open cylinders, their alpha fading up, with world-space scanlines
                 that scroll
               - a pool of light on the sand
               - the hologram
               - the lines, the waiting and leaving, the camera shots
               - the ending card text, saves
  The hologram:
    - One Actor, drawn without the sketch shader, so an Actor from a BAM skins on the CPU (as in
      Utopia Vision).
    - It is drawn in two layers that share it as an instance (skinned once):
        - a body, plain alpha-blended: it reads as cyan even against the bright noon sky
          (Crimson is poisoned by day, so Gleebs usually comes by day)
        - an additive glow over it, strong at night
    - Its own textures are switched off for scanlines generated from world height.
    - If the model cannot load, the beam, the words and the ending still work.
  main.py:
    - world_frozen() also holds while leaving / after leaving
    - _place_camera() hands the camera to the scene while it plays
  crimson.py's third soft light carries Gleebs' cyan light once Crimson is dead.
  Sounds (placeholder, synthesised by tools_make_placeholder_audio.py): lore_read, gleebs_arrive,
  gleebs_voice, gleebs_depart.
  Cost: the etchings update in 0.02 ms a frame. Gleebs loads only when it comes.
