“What are you doing?”

“Keeping the door.”

“You just said—”

“Not for him.”

The hidden structure moved beyond it. Somewhere inside, a person tapped four times. Paused. Then tapped four more. Gleebs answered with his fingers against the kitchen counter. Not an agreement. A signal that someone was still present.
