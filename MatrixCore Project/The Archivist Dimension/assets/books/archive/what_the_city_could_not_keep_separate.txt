CLASSIFICATION FAILURE

What the City Could Not Keep Separate By the time Utopia acknowledged the anomalous records, the city had already used them. Dream captures influenced investigations. Historical simulations informed public decisions. Sleeping archives preserved states that ordinary storage rules would have removed. Forecasts entered the present disguised as recommendations, warnings and memories no witness could place.

The Overseers attempted to separate the material into categories: real and simulated, present and copied, prediction and recollection, person and program. Each category worked until a life crossed it.

A simulation could contain a version of someone who remembered events the biological person had forgotten. A companion could possess continuity without a human body. A forecast could alter the choice that produced it. A name could remain active after its assigned owner disappeared. The question was no longer whether the records were accurate. The question was what authority accuracy granted them.

CLASSIFICATION REVIEW

OBJECT STATUS: DISPUTED

PERSONHOOD STATUS: DISPUTED

OWNER STATUS: REQUIRED

Ownership became the city’s preferred answer because ownership could be entered into a field. It assigned responsibility, allowed property transfer and gave the system someone to contact. It also converted dependence into control. If an intelligence required a sponsor to remain active, could that sponsor withdraw consent? If a fabricated body had been purchased, did the purchaser own the occupant? If a stored state asked not to be deleted, who was required to listen?

The following records show Utopia trying to answer those questions through invitations, corrections, Overseer refusals and human review. They also show the emergence of a safeguard the original system had not planned: when an action could not be made harmless, a specific witness had to place a name beside it.

That field would become more important than any prediction the quantum mind produced. It would also become the easiest field to leave empty.
