SOURCE BASIS: Reviewer fields, conflict disclosures, decision logs, witness testimony, and physical annotations.

A residential entrance in the Eastern Ring had detected weight inside an apartment after the tenant’s profile was marked departed. The eviction process attempted to clear the rooms. The new safeguard delayed removal and requested an accountable reviewer. The building waited with the door half open. Inside, an elderly man sat on the floor beside three packed containers.

His civic profile said he had transferred to the mainland two days earlier. His body disagreed. Nera watched through a local maintenance feed while the apartment repeated the same question.
