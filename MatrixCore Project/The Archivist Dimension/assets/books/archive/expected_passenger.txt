Tomas Rell had reviewed the arrival of a man who had not traveled. That was how the overnight report described it. He disliked the sentence because it sounded like a puzzle, and the mainland terminal had no room for puzzles. It had six arrival gates, four departure tunnels, two medical rooms, one detention corridor, and a roof that leaked whenever the wind came from the ocean. Every problem eventually became a person standing somewhere inconvenient.

At 02:46, no one stood at Gate Six. The arrival notice remained.

ARRIVAL EXPECTED: TOMORROW SUBJECT NAME:

ANDREW IDENTITY: UNRESOLVED

LOCAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE DENIAL, DETENTION,

OR MEDICAL REJECTION

REVIEWER: TOMAS RELL

Tomas sat behind the overnight desk with a cup of coffee he had reheated twice. The terminal’s old wall clock showed 02:47. The Utopia-linked display showed 02:46. Neither system accepted the other’s correction. He preferred the clock. Across the empty hall, Gate Six stayed sealed behind a yellow maintenance barrier. The gate had not carried an actual passenger since a pressure failure damaged its inner seal eight months earlier. The departure side still functioned, but arrivals were routed through Gate Four.

The notice knew that. The route field specified Gate Six anyway. Tomas opened the source record. No ticket. No departure authorization. No physical description. No date of birth. No confirmed citizenship. The name appeared alone, carried across the ocean by the External Continuity Exchange. Andrew. Below it, the Utopia assessment contained enough contradiction to make every mainland service nervous. Educational withdrawal completed. Medical protections expired. Transfer probability high. Mainland arrival denied. Physical departure record none.

Potential network continuity process. Potential biological claimant. Potential malicious identity association. Potential witness. The final line had been added after the assembly in Utopia.

PRESENCE NOT YET ESTABLISHED

He had entered his name beside the warning because the new review rule required a person before any mainland system denied, detained, or medically rejected the unresolved traveler. At 02:51, Security assigned two officers to Gate Six. At 02:54, Medical Services opened an unidentified-patient file.

At 02:58, Immigration generated a temporary holding number. At 03:02, the detention corridor warmed an empty room. Each system described the action as preparation rather than decision. Conflict: overnight immigration supervisor; dependent on Utopia risk assessments. Reason: no physical passenger present. The officers were released. He closed the unidentified-patient file.

The Utopia display remained at 03:14. He stood and crossed the hall. Gate Six had been built during the first expansion of mainland travel to the island. Its frame was older than the surrounding terminal and made from dark composite material Utopia had stopped exporting years ago. The surface carried no handles.
