# The Archivist 3D — Pass 07: Planetary Story Systems

## Summary
Pass 07 converts the free-floating record field from Pass 06 into a navigable
cosmology. The archive remains a zero-gravity, floorless, observer-centered 4D
space, but the 55 preserved records now communicate story hierarchy through
scale, orbit, and motion.

## Planetary archive structure
- 6 major story planets: MatrixCore, Afterlife of IO, Entropy, Utopia,
  Apocalypse, and The Archive.
- 45 related written records orbit their parent worlds as story moons.
- 4 linked non-primary records appear as cybernetic simulation satellites.
- 4 linked primary planets additionally receive a separate mechanical simulation
  gate in orbit, so a major story can remain the story planet while its playable
  reality reads as a different object.
- The one non-catalog test link remains ignored by the visible 55-record archive.

Every catalog record is assigned exactly once. There are no duplicate or
unassigned story records.

## Scale and motion
- Primary story planets are substantially larger than side-story moons.
- Orb radius also receives a bounded contribution from the real source-story
  text length, so longer records read as slightly more massive without creating
  disruptive size outliers.
- Moons and simulation satellites use true inclined 3D orbit planes rather than
  merely rotating their object orientation.
- Faint system orbit tracks provide navigation cues without becoming a floor or
  enclosing room.
- Same-system records are favored by the existing constellation relationship
  display.

## Preserved systems
- Pass 06 six-direction zero-gravity movement and damping.
- Observer-centered moving 4D exterior.

- Story reading / reading progress.

- Reality Bleed.
- Nested HoloVerse dimension entry / return.
