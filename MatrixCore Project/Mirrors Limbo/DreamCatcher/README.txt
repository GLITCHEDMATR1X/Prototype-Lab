DREAMCATCHER — P106 (part of Mirror's Limbo)

DreamCatcher lives inside the Mirror's Limbo folder and uses Limbo's shared gx_common
package. Keep it there. Andrew's Nightmare is nested at modes/andrews_nightmare.

START
Normally reached from Mirror's Limbo: roadside TV -> E -> DREAMCATCHER.
RUN_DREAMCATCHER.bat still starts the house directly for testing.

TRAVEL
Andrew's Nightmare: E at the bedroom TV/chair, Left/Right to CH 07 DREAM, then T.
Mirror's Limbo: ESC -> RETURN TO MIRROR'S LIMBO. After the attic finale, ESC (or 10 s)
wakes you in Limbo and the house is recorded as cleared.

PROGRESS AND SETTINGS
House progress is saved whenever you leave (Andrew, Limbo or desktop) and restored on
return. Finishing the finale clears it for a fresh next visit.
Display mode, resolution, v-sync, mouse sensitivity, invert-Y, master volume and the FOV
preference follow Mirror's Limbo settings. Changes made here are written back to Limbo.
