GLYPHBOUND MUSIC REPLACEMENT
============================

Drop .wav, .ogg, or .mp3 tracks directly in this folder.

Filenames do not matter.

ONE TRACK
The track loops continuously.

MULTIPLE TRACKS
Tracks are shuffled at startup and play one after another.

You can replace all music simply by deleting the audio files in this folder
and dropping in your own. Keep this one music folder; no duplicate folders are needed.

If the folder contains no playable audio, Glyphbound simply runs without music.

READ SAFETY (PASS 08)
Unreadable, truncated, or unsupported tracks are skipped once and quarantined for
the rest of that run. The game will not repeatedly retry a bad track. WAV and OGG
are the most portable choices across Panda3D audio backends; MP3 support can depend
on the installed Panda3D audio backend/codec support.
